Outcome Led

The strongest spine is the decision, not the feature tour.

Metric question

Show me degraded towers.

A dashboard can expose part of the picture: status, utilization, dropped calls, and repair history.

Decision question

Which towers should we upgrade within the 5 million dollar budget?

  • Which towers are degraded or likely to fail?
  • Which customers and calls are affected?
  • Which upgrades restore the most capacity per dollar?
  • Which plan fits budget and crew constraints?

Payoff: a feasible upgrade plan with an explanation, not just a ranked report.
